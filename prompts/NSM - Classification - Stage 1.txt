- "Great explanation! Thank you :)"

Questions or concerns about the accuracy of difficulty levels assigned to problems, particularly when there's a perceived mismatch between the rating and actual student performance data.

3. "Video Solution request":
Requests from students for video explanations of specific problems, typically for complex questions or when they need additional visual guidance to understand the solution approach.

Examples:
- "Can the team please provide a video solution to this question."
- "Would really appreciate if a video solution could be made for this question."
- "Solution is not clear, please upload a video if possible."
